## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, ergonomic, and dependable UI language for agro-industrial packing plants, cold storage facilities, and agricultural traceability desks. The environment demands immediate clarity under harsh industrial lighting, rapid physical operations, and touch-screen accessibility where operators frequently interact with heavy equipment, barcode scanners, and scales.

### Design Principles
- **Clarity Under Pressure:** Essential metrics—such as batch weights, pallet IDs, and temperature thresholds—must be readable from a distance and at glancing angles.
- **Organic Robustness:** Balance the earthy, trustworthy authority of cold-chain agriculture with modern, precision-engineered software design.
- **Ergonomic Tactility:** Generous interactive hit targets and distinct affordances suited for industrial tablets, touch kiosks, and hand-held terminal scanning.
- **Structured Density:** Information-dense tables and inventory grids maintain rhythmic whitespace, mitigating visual exhaustion over long operational shifts.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-efficiency logistics in deep botanical tones, balanced with clean technical neutrals and specific functional cues for cold-chain monitoring.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#143D32` / `#1B4D3E`):** Deep Forest Green. Anchors navigation shells, primary actions, heavy headers, and systemic authority. Evokes agricultural legacy and solid industrial governance.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#059669` / `#10B981`):** Fresh Emerald. Conveys active statuses, verified inspections, optimal fruit freshness, and primary action highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7`):** Cold Storage Blue. Dedicated to refrigeration tracking, cold tunnel metrics, temperature logging, and chilling status indicators.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - **Cold/Maturity Warning:** Cold Amber (`#F59E0B`) indicates temperature drift, maturity index flags, or re-inspection alerts.
  - **Critical Alert / Loss:** Soft Crimson (`#EF4444`) handles rejected batches, phytosanitary quarantine stops, and system drop-offs.
  - **Fruit Categorization:** Subdued contextual pastels paired with high-contrast text:
    - *Cherry:* Deep Rose Tint (`#FFE4E6` background, `#9F1239` text)
    - *Grape:* Soft Violet Tint (`#EDE9FE` background, `#5B21B6` text)
    - *Apple:* Fresh Mint Tint (`#DCFCE7` background, `#166534` text)
    - *Kiwi:* Warm Olive Tint (`#FEF9C3` background, `#854D0E` text)
- **Surfaces & Neutrals:** Foundation utilizes pure white (`#FFFFFF`) cards layered across light slate backdrops (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`) with structural hairline borders (`#E2E8F0`).

## Typography

Typography prioritizes legibility and swift scanning. **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides structural character and modern authority for headings and module titles. **Inter** handles high-volume operational body text, labels, and table entries.

### Tabular Figures & Numerics
All weight readings (kg/lbs), batch codes, cold tunnel temperatures (°C), and traceability IDs strictly utilize tabular lining numerals (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1`). This eliminates layout shift during real-time scale syncs and maintains perfect vertical alignment down dense ledger tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs an adaptable fluid grid tailored for rugged 10-inch tablets on forklifts, packhouse floor terminals, and multi-monitor management stations.

### Grid Foundations
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile / Scanner Handheld (< 640px):** 4 columns, `margin`: `1rem`, `gutter`: `1rem`. Optimized for single-thumb scanning, linear inspection forms, and vertical pallet feeds.
  - **Tablet / Industrial Terminals (640px – 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin`: `1.5rem`, `gutter`: `1.5rem`. Split-screen view for barcode entry and live inventory updates.
  - **Desktop / Plant Monitoring (> 1024px):** 12 columns, `margin`: `2rem`, `gutter`: `1.5rem`. Max content container capped at `1600px` for wide traceability spreadsheets.

### Ergonomic Spacing Rules
Packing areas require touch target safety. Critical actions enforce a minimum tap clearance of 48px × 48px, surrounded by `space-sm` (8px) margins to prevent accidental input when operators wear industrial gloves.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages high-precision tonal layering, crisp borders, and subtle diffuse shadows to prevent visual mud in bright warehouse environments.

### Tonal Architecture
- **Base Canvas:** Neutral cool tint (`#F8FAFC`) to minimize glare under industrial fluorescent and LED fixtures.
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards, Tables, Panels):** Solid white (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px hairline perimeter borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Surface Level 2 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Action Bars):** Solid white with elevated ambient shadows (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(20, 61, 50, 0.08)`).
- **Interactive Depths:**
  - Standard containers rely on `border: 1px solid #E2E8F0` rather than heavy shadows.
  - Active scanning/focus rings employ a 2px tinted halo using Secondary 400 (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`).
  - Drag-and-drop pallet states elevate with a directional drop shadow tinted with deep forest tone: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(20, 61, 50, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric corners with refined radius smoothing to establish an industrial yet technologically advanced feel. 

Containers, cards, and data surfaces feature standard radius shaping (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem for master module containers, `rounded-lg` / 1rem for internal cards, and `0.5rem` for inputs and buttons). Form inputs, scan fields, and filter chips balance soft ergonomics with structural discipline. Badges and status pills leverage full pill contours (`rounded-full`) for instant shape differentiation against rectangular metrics cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep forest green background (`#143D32`), white text, bold font (`label-lg`), minimum height of `48px` for plant ergonomics. Hover states darken to `#0F2F27`.
- **Secondary:** Surface white (`#FFFFFF`), border `1.5px solid #143D32`, text `#143D32`. Active click states flash emerald tint (`#ECFDF5`).
- **Accent / Confirm:** Emerald green (`#059669`) background with white text for quick validation, scale acceptances, and lot closures.

### Data Tables
- **Grid Density:** Compact headers with uppercase micro-labels (`label-sm`, tracking `0.04em`), subtle horizontal separators (`#F1F5F9`), and zebra striping turned off in favor of hover transitions (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Numeric Cells:** Right-aligned, utilizing tabular font styling with dynamic color cues (e.g., Cold chamber temperature `#0284C7`, threshold alerts `#F59E0B`).

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Quality Tiers:**
  - *Exportación:* Emerald tone badge (`#ECFDF5` background, `#065F46` border and text).
  - *Local:* Neutral blue tone badge (`#F0F9FF` background, `#0369A1` border and text).
  - *Industrial:* Warm slate tone badge (`#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` border and text).
- **Fruit Varieties:** Subdued background tints with corresponding botanical text (Cherry: Rose, Grape: Violet, Apple: Green, Kiwi: Olive) accompanied by a distinct 6px colored indicator dot.

### Barcode & RFID Integrated Input Fields
- **Architecture:** High-contrast text field with a permanent integrated scanner icon and an active listening state (pulsing border in `#10B981` with subtle badge tag "RFID ACTIVE").
- **Metrics Display:** Numerical entry fields for scales integrate unit suffixes (`KG`, `LBS`, `PALLET`) locked to the right margin in bold tabular typography.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Minimum dimension `20px × 20px` with a 2px active border. In selected states, checkboxes use `#143D32` with an emerald verification checkmark.
